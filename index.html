[ THE STORY ]

JANUARY 12, 2021: THE DAY FLASH DIED

On January 12, 2021, Adobe triggered a hardcoded time-bomb killswitch inside the Flash Player plugin. Overnight, twenty-five years of interactive browser history and creator culture were bricked behind a silent warning dialog.

📰 THE SILENT CORPORATE TIME-BOMB

Millions of childhood memories—from the martial arts stickman choreography of Xiao Xiao to the snowy plazas of Club Penguin and physics doodles of Line Rider—instantly ceased to function. The web was told to move on.

Proprietary runtimes showed their true nature: when a company decides a format is over, creators lose access to their own creative history.

🐧 Club Penguin (2005–2017) 🥋 Xiao Xiao (2001–2004) 🛷 Line Rider (2006–2021) 👖 Fancy Pants (2006–2021) 👽 Alien Hominid (2002–2021)
⚡ THE RESURRECTION ENGINE

KillSwitch.swf turns this tragic date into an interactive arena. You play as an indie challenger who breaches the 2021 kill-switch dialog to fight the Ghost of Flash Player inside a recreated Macromedia Flash Player 10.3 desktop window.

[ ARCHITECTURE ]

HOW IT WORKS: DUAL-LAYER AI ENGINE

Most fighting game bots rely on static rule trees or scripted loops. KillSwitch.swf combines a high-speed 60 FPS deterministic Q-learning engine for split-second martial arts tactics with Google Gemma 3:4B for inter-round habit roasts and strategic weight shifts.

+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+ | LAYER 1: REAL-TIME 60 FPS COMBAT REINFORCEMENT LEARNER (Deterministic Q-Table) | | • State Space: Distance Bucket (close <75px, mid 75-155px, far) x Class (air / block / attack / idle) | | • 10 Atomic Behaviors: EVADE, ANTI_AIR, WHIFF_PUNISH, GUARD_BREAK, PUNCH_CHAIN, KICK_CHAIN, | | UPPERCUT, FLYING_KNEE, JUMP_IN, RETREAT | | • Pre-Trained Weights: Bootstrapped on 1,500 simulated rounds across 5 bot archetypes (<200 KB JSON) | +-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+ | v (Round Over / Knockout) +-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+ | LAYER 2: STRATEGIC INTER-ROUND ADAPTATION & HABIT ROASTS (Local Google Gemma 3:4B via Ollama) | | • Telemetry Streamed: aerialRatio, turtleRatio, Markov move sequence bigrams | | • Authentic Monologue: Generates in-character Newgrounds roasts typed out letter-by-letter | | • Strategic Reparameterization: Tunes antiAirPriority, aggression, guardBreakRate, and 0x7E5 Rage | +-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

Layer 1: Real-Time Q-Learning

60 FPS TACTICS

The in-fight brain chooses one of 10 atomic martial arts behaviors every decision interval based on temporal difference rewards:

  • Damage Efficiency Rewards: Positive rewards for landing strikes, heavy penalties for whiffing or taking clean hits.
  • Habit Interception: Combines Q-values with Markov sequence predictions to parry expected heavy kicks.
  • Anti-Passivity Rule: When behind on HP with <50% round time, consecutive retreats/evasions are strictly forbidden.

Layer 2: Local Google Gemma 3:4B

STRATEGIC MEMORY

Between rounds, full combat telemetry is analyzed by open-weight Gemma 3:4B running 100% offline via Ollama:

  • Adaptive Weight Shifting: If you spam jump-kicks, Gemma ramps antiAirPriority to 0.95; if you turtle, it primes guardBreakRate.
  • In-Character Spoken Roasts: Spoken character-by-character in retro CRT typewriter font, dripping with authentic 2000s Newgrounds lore.
  • ActionScript Error 0x7E5: Triggers the terrifying triple-teleport boss rage mechanic when the Ghost drops below 35% HP.

⚖️ UNIVERSAL FAIR-PLAY ARCHITECTURE

No Same-Frame Parries: Defensive reactions enforce a mandatory 2–3 frame delay. Ghost never parries on frame 0 of player input.
25-Frame Block Cooldown: Blocks incur recovery frames so the AI cannot turtle indefinitely against cancel chains.
Dynamic Comeback Assist (Hidden): If the player drops 2 rounds in a row, next round reaction delay eases by +4f and mistake rate by +8%.
Non-Stacking Round Scaling: Round bonuses cycle between Damage, HP, and Speed (+4%/rd, max +25%). Stats never stack simultaneously.
